# The Return of the King - Scouring, "Sharkey", and athelas

After the War, the hobbits find the Shire under petty tyranny: "gatherers and sharers," extra Shirriffs, and mills belching smoke. Lotho "Pimple" Sackville-Baggins fronted the takeover, but Saruman (now called "Sharkey") pulls the strings. Wormtongue slays Saruman at Bag End and is shot by hobbit archers, closing a thread entirely missing from most adaptations.

Healer's token: earlier in Minas Tirith, the old rhyme, "the hands of the king are the hands of a healer" proves true when Aragorn uses athelas (kingsfoil) to draw Éowyn, Faramir, and Merry back from shadow. The herb gives little help unless wielded by the rightful king, another detail that rewards close reading.
