# The Fellowship of the Ring - The Barrow-blades

After Tom Bombadil rescues the hobbits in the Barrow-downs, he arms them with ancient blades from Westernesse (Cardolan). These "barrow-blades" were forged with malice against the Witch-king. Much later on in Battle of the Pelennor Fields, Merry's strike with one of these blades helps unbind the Nazgûl's protections, enabling Éowyn's finishing blow. The episode in the barrow and the origin of the blades is absent from most adaptations, which is why the hobbit weapon matters in the book's logic.

Obscure note: the blades' provenance ties to the old kingdoms of Arthedain, Cardolan, and Rhudaur; their craft specifically harms the Witch-king of Angmar.
