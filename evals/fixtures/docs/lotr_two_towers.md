# The Two Towers - Quickbeam, rowans, and Huorns

During the Entmoot, the young Ent Bregalad ("Quickbeam") befriends Merry and Pippin and shows them a grove of rowans felled by Isengard's orcs. Quickbeam's unusual "hastiness" contrasts with the Ents' slow deliberation and helps turn the meeting toward marching on Isengard. The grief over the rowans personalises the Ents' choice.

Book-only layers: "Huorns" (tree-herds without full speech) later close around the fleeing orcs after Helm's Deep and by morning, none remain. This quiet retribution is largely minimised or omitted in screen versions.
